# Human reference set (exploratory, 2026-11-05)

Not pre-registered as a bar, but the selection rule was fixed before any paper was downloaded or scored.

- **Question.** Where do human-written robot-learning papers from before November 2022 score on the open EditLens 3B model, by section, when text is taken from a PDF in the same way as our own? This calibrates the scale the paper uses and says whether a floor near 0.15 on result paragraphs is a property of our writing or of the detector.
- **Selection rule.** arXiv API, `cat:cs.RO AND (abs:imitation OR abs:"world model" OR abs:"reward model" OR abs:"failure detection" OR abs:visuomotor)`, `submittedDate` 2018-01-01 to 2021-12-31, sorted by relevance. The first 40 results, in the order returned, with no manual choice (`expected/selected.json`, the query date 2026-11-05). A paper is dropped only if the extractor finds fewer than 8 scoreable paragraphs, and every dropped paper is listed in the paper.
- **Texts.** The PDFs are fetched from arxiv.org (3 s between requests) into `~/scratch/WS-162/refs_human_2026-11-05/pdfs/` and read with `pdftotext` in reading order, references dropped (`paper_map.pdf_sections`). Our four papers go through the same extractor from their built `main.pdf`. Paragraphs of at least 40 words are scored one at a time; the text is not stored here, only the arXiv id, section, word count, score and a hash.
- **Section type** is read from the heading (abstract, introduction, related work, method, results, conclusion, other) by the regular expressions in `scripts/score_pdfs.py`.
- **Outcome.** Mean and median paragraph score by section type for the human set and for ours, the paper-level means, and the share of human paragraphs above 0.15 and 0.25.
- **Use of the model.** The model is licensed for non-commercial use and the owner restricts it to his own papers. The human papers are used as a calibration set only, as the three pre-2022 references of WS-162 were. No score is reported or used as a statement about a named paper or its authors.
- **Known limits.** Forty papers from one query are not a sample of the field. PDF extraction adds noise (hyphenation, captions, two-column order) that differs by paper. The papers are from before November 2022, so they cannot hold text from current assistants, but a few may have been edited with earlier tools. Our papers are compared by the same extractor, which is different from the markdown the earlier tables use.
