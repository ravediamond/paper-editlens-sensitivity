# Triggers: what the open EditLens score reacts to (exploratory, 2026-11-05)

Not pre-registered. Two batteries of controlled edits on host paragraphs.

- **First battery** (`expected/battery.json`): hosts chosen by their score, 120 human paragraphs below 0.03, 90 above 0.20, and our 62. It is not used for the paper's numbers. Choosing hosts at the ends of the score makes any edit look like it raises the low hosts and lowers the high ones (regression to the mean): for example appending a neutral sentence lowered the high hosts by 0.17.
- **Second battery** (`expected/battery2.json`, `scripts/trigger_battery2.py`): 100 random human paragraphs (60 to 250 words, back matter excluded, seed 11) and our 62 paragraphs. Edits: a real sentence from another paragraph of the same collection appended and prepended (the control), a sentence in the style of a language model (appended and prepended), a generic "important and challenging problem" opener, a generic importance sentence, a neutral figure pointer (appended and prepended), a long specific technical human paragraph (appended and prepended) and a paragraph of the same register, names replaced by "the method", digits dropped, and evaluative words deleted. The outcome is the score of the edited paragraph minus the score of the original, per host.
- **Reading.** An effect counts relative to its control: for a human host the control changes the score by 0.025 (appended) or 0.014 (prepended) downward, so a sentence that does the same is not a trigger.
- **Attribution** (`scripts/saliency.py`): gradient of the score with respect to the input embeddings times the embedding, per word, on nine example paragraphs. Only the share of the positive attribution held by the top tenth of the words is stored (`expected/saliency_summary.json`); the per-word values contain paper text and stay out of the repository.
- **Known limits.** The edits are examples we chose. Each edits one thing at a time on paragraphs that were not written for it. The language-model-style sentence is one sentence.
