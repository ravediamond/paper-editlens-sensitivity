# human-dose

Human edits of AI-drafted paragraphs against AI edits of human paragraphs, at the same edit magnitude: does the open EditLens 3B score
fall as much as it rises? The human rows come from the owner's hand rewrites of the four papers (each commit a version); the AI rows from
the authors' own dataset. `PROTOCOL.md` is frozen before any number; `expected/` holds the rows once they exist; `REPRODUCE.md` the commands.
